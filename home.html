Why It Works

How We Train

⚡
Phosphate System Only

Short, max-intensity reps with full recovery. No grinding, no junk volume. We build the sprint reservoir first — quality over quantity, always.

🏁
ArenaGear FAT Timing

Every sprint electronically measured to the hundredth. Athletes see their MPH after every rep. Hand timing doesn't cut it when you're chasing hundredths.

📈
Record, Rank, Publish

Times ranked and published every session. Every athlete has a number. Speed grows like a tree — we track every inch of development all season.

😎
Happy Athletes Run Faster

Culture is the product. Athletes who love training compete better and stay injury-free. We train cats — not cogs in a conditioning machine.
