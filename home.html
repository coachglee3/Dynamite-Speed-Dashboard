Questions

FAQs

What age groups are eligible?

All programs are open to athletes ages 6–18. Youth athletes (U6–U12) follow age-appropriate modifications on every drill. High school athletes train in a separate program structure.

Do I need prior track or football experience?

No experience required. We start every new athlete with a baseline assessment on Day 1 — timed sprints and a Triple Broad Jump. Your first number is the starting point. Everything goes up from there.

What is the AG510 timing system?

The ArenaGear AG510 is a fully automatic timing (FAT) system — the same category of technology used in elite track and field competitions. Every sprint rep is electronically measured to the hundredth of a second. No hand timing, no guesswork.

Can parents watch times in real time?

Yes. Download the ArenaGear Teams app on iOS or Google Play. No account required — connect directly to our session and watch your athlete's times live from the sideline.

Is this a conditioning camp?

Absolutely not. We do not train fatigue. We train speed, movement, and performance. Sprint work is short and explosive with full recovery between reps. Tired athletes go home. Tired is the enemy.

When is payment due?

Payment is collected on site at the session — cash or your preferred method. You book your spot online to reserve your place; payment happens when you arrive. No pre-payment required to hold a spot.

Can my athlete attend just one session?

Yes. Rolling enrollment means there is no contract or minimum commitment. Join a single session, try it out, and come back when it works for your schedule. Spots are limited per session — booking ahead is recommended.

Where can I see my athlete's progress over time?

Every athlete receives a Roster ID and can log into the Athlete Dashboard to view their full speed history, MPH trends, 40Y dash times, and Triple Broad Jump progression across the entire season.
